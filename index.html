Pip Phonics: Learn to Read

Systematic phonics for children aged 3 to 7: letter sounds, blending, sight words, magic e, vowel teams and decodable books, with Pip the chick.

FAQ

Where does my child start? Set the level during setup or later in the grown-ups area (the lock button on the map). Earlier lessons stay open for practice.

What is free? All of unit 1 (s, a, t, p, i, n, blending and the first book) is free forever, no card needed.

How do I cancel my subscription? Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad → your name → Subscriptions → Pip Phonics → Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid a charge. Pip also reminds you two days before a free trial ends.

Do I pay for each child or device? No. One purchase covers unlimited children and, with Family Sharing, your family's devices.

How do I restore my purchase? Grown-ups area → Restore purchases.

How does "Read aloud to Pip" work? When you turn it on in the grown-ups area, Pip listens while your child reads a word or a book page. Speech is understood on the device; nothing is recorded, stored or sent. A missed word is never marked wrong.

There's no sound. Turn up the volume and check that the device isn't connected to Bluetooth headphones. Pip plays sound even when the silent switch is on.
