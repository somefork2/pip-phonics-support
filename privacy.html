Privacy Policy

Pip Phonics: Learn to Read (“the app”). Effective date: November 1, 2026. Applies to the iPhone and iPad app.

Summary

The app is made for children. We collect no data at all: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party software development kits.

Data stored on the device

A child's first name, age, chosen animal buddy, lesson progress, stickers, and any word list a grown-up types in are stored only on the device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting a child in the grown-ups area, or deleting the app, erases this data.

Microphone and speech recognition

If a grown-up turns on “Read aloud to Pip”, the app uses the microphone while the child reads, and Apple's on-device speech recognition to check the words. Audio is processed on the device only, is never recorded or stored, and is never sent to us or to Apple's servers. The feature is off until a grown-up turns it on behind the parental gate.

Notifications

With a grown-up's permission the app schedules local reminders (a daily reading reminder and a reminder before a free trial ends). They are created on the device; nothing leaves it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. We never receive payment details; the app only checks with the App Store whether access is active. Purchases and links out of the app are behind a parental gate.

Children

The app is designed for children and follows the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the App Store Kids Category rules. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to share, sell or delete. Parents can contact us with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
